### Statement of Facts  
1. The notice alleges that the input tax credit (ITC) of **Rs 38,904.00** claimed in Table 4(A)(5) of FORM GSTR‑3B for December 2025 **exceeds** the auto‑populated credit in FORM GSTR‑2B because the relevant suppliers did **not** report the invoices in their FORM GSTR‑1 and there is a mismatch in taxable values. [General]  

2. The taxpayer’s books contain the **tax invoices** issued by the said suppliers, the tax has been **paid** to the Government, and the invoices have been reflected in the taxpayer’s GSTR‑3B return. [Client document] – [ATTACH: supplier‑wise invoice register]  

3. The supplier’s GSTR‑1 filings do not show these invoices, which is the sole reason for the difference observed in GSTR‑2B. [General] – [ATTACH: supplier GSTR‑1 extraction]
