ACME CORPORATION
ANNUAL REPORT — FISCAL YEAR 2023
Form 10-K Filing


ITEM 1. BUSINESS

Acme Corporation (NASDAQ: ACME) is a diversified technology and manufacturing conglomerate
headquartered in San Francisco, California.  Founded in 1987, the company operates through
three principal business segments: Enterprise Software, Advanced Manufacturing, and Financial
Services.

During fiscal year 2023, Acme Corporation continued to execute on its long-term strategic
plan to transition from hardware-centric revenue streams to high-margin recurring software
and services revenue.  The company completed the acquisition of DataBridge Analytics in
March 2023 for a total consideration of $1.4 billion, which is expected to be accretive to
earnings per share by fiscal year 2025.

ITEM 1A. RISK FACTORS

The company faces significant competition in each of its markets.  Key risk factors include:

- Macroeconomic uncertainty and potential global recession risks that could reduce enterprise
  technology spending.
- Increasing competition from well-capitalized cloud-native competitors including major
  hyperscalers.
- Cybersecurity threats and data privacy regulatory risks, particularly GDPR compliance in
  Europe and CCPA in California.
- Supply chain disruptions affecting manufacturing segment input costs.
- Foreign currency exchange rate fluctuations, with approximately 38% of revenues derived
  from international markets.

ITEM 7. MANAGEMENT'S DISCUSSION AND ANALYSIS OF FINANCIAL CONDITION

RESULTS OF OPERATIONS — FISCAL YEAR 2023

Revenue

Total revenue for fiscal year 2023 was $12.4 billion, representing an increase of 8.7%
compared to $11.4 billion in fiscal year 2022.  Software and services revenue grew 18.3%
to $7.2 billion, while hardware revenue declined 4.1% to $5.2 billion, consistent with
our strategic shift towards higher-margin recurring revenue streams.

International revenue grew 11.2% to $4.7 billion, representing 37.9% of total revenue.
The Enterprise Software segment contributed $4.8 billion in revenue, up 22.1% year-over-year,
driven by strong cloud adoption and expansion of our SaaS platform.  Advanced Manufacturing
revenue was $5.1 billion, down 1.8% due to softer demand in consumer electronics end markets.
Financial Services revenue was $2.5 billion, up 15.3%, driven by increased transaction
volumes and new customer acquisitions.

Operating Expenses

Total operating expenses for fiscal year 2023 were $9.8 billion, compared to $9.1 billion
in fiscal year 2022.  Cost of revenue was $5.9 billion, resulting in gross profit of
$6.5 billion and a gross margin of 52.4%, up from 49.1% in fiscal year 2022.

Research and development expenses increased 14.2% to $1.8 billion, or 14.5% of revenue,
reflecting continued investment in next-generation AI and machine learning capabilities.

Sales and marketing expenses were $1.4 billion, representing 11.3% of revenue, relatively
flat compared to 11.8% in fiscal year 2022 as we achieve scale leverage on our go-to-market
investment.

General and administrative expenses were $0.7 billion, or 5.6% of revenue, down from 6.1%
in the prior year.

Operating Income and EBITDA

Operating income for fiscal year 2023 was $2.6 billion, up 31.3% from $2.0 billion in fiscal
year 2022.  Operating margin expanded to 21.0% from 17.5% in the prior year.

Adjusted EBITDA for fiscal year 2023 was $3.4 billion, representing an adjusted EBITDA margin
of 27.4%, compared to $2.6 billion and 22.8% in fiscal year 2022.  The improvement in
profitability reflects the favorable revenue mix shift toward software and services, as well
as operational efficiency initiatives that reduced our cost structure by approximately
$200 million.

Net Income and Earnings Per Share

Net income attributable to Acme Corporation stockholders for fiscal year 2023 was $1.9 billion,
or $3.82 per diluted share, compared to $1.4 billion, or $2.79 per diluted share, in fiscal
year 2022.  This represents a 36.9% increase in net income and a 36.9% increase in diluted
earnings per share.

The effective tax rate for fiscal year 2023 was 21.3%, compared to 21.8% in fiscal year 2022.

ITEM 9A. CONTROLS AND PROCEDURES

Management's Annual Report on Internal Control Over Financial Reporting

Acme Corporation's management, under the supervision of the Chief Executive Officer and
Chief Financial Officer, is responsible for establishing and maintaining adequate internal
control over financial reporting as defined in Rules 13a-15(f) and 15d-15(f) under the
Securities Exchange Act of 1934.

Our internal control over financial reporting is designed to provide reasonable assurance
regarding the reliability of financial reporting and the preparation of financial statements
for external purposes in accordance with generally accepted accounting principles (GAAP).

Based on our evaluation, management concluded that as of December 31, 2023, Acme Corporation's
internal control over financial reporting is effective.


NOTES TO CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS

Note 1 — Summary of Significant Accounting Policies

Revenue Recognition: The company recognizes revenue in accordance with ASC 606.  Software
license revenue is recognized at the point in time when the license is delivered to the
customer.  SaaS subscription revenue is recognized ratably over the subscription term.
Hardware revenue is recognized upon shipment or delivery depending on contractual terms.

Note 5 — Acquisitions

DataBridge Analytics: On March 14, 2023, the company acquired 100% of the outstanding shares
of DataBridge Analytics, Inc., a provider of AI-powered financial data analytics, for total
consideration of $1.4 billion, comprised of $1.1 billion in cash and $0.3 billion in Acme
common stock.  The acquisition added approximately $180 million of annualised revenue and is
expected to accelerate development of the company's AI/ML platform capabilities.

Note 8 — Debt

As of December 31, 2023, the company had total debt outstanding of $4.3 billion, comprised
of $500 million in short-term borrowings and $3.8 billion in long-term notes.  The weighted
average interest rate on outstanding debt was 4.2%.  The company is in compliance with all
debt covenants.
